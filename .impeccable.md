## Design Context

### Users
Microsoft Point of Contact viewing a final-deliverable presentation from a student team. They are professional, will notice quality immediately, and are evaluating both the research findings AND the team's execution. The website is used live during a presentation — it needs to command a room and hold up to scrutiny on a large screen.

### Brand Personality
- **Three words:** Expedition. Precise. Playful.
- Not a startup dashboard. Not a student science fair. A mission briefing that you'd actually want to look at.
- Emotional goal: the viewer should feel like they're being brought into a well-run research mission. Credible, not boring. Fun to navigate, not sloppy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** Space exploration — but executed as "mission control" / "star chart", not "video game lobby" or "Discord server dark mode". Think NASA mission patch meets Wired magazine data spread.
- **Two galaxies only:** (1) Explore the Data (experienced vs. newcomers), (2) Prompt Libraries
- **Dark theme:** Deep cosmic navy (`oklch(12% 0.025 248)`) — dark but clearly space-tinted, never pure black
- **Anti-references:** No neon glow maximalism. No pure black + cyan/purple gradients (the AI aesthetic). No rounded cards with generic drop shadows. Nothing that looks "vibecoded".
- **Color palette:**
  - Background: `oklch(12% 0.025 248)` deep navy
  - Surfaces: `oklch(18% 0.02 248)` panel navy
  - Text: `oklch(93% 0.008 248)` near-white, cool-tinted
  - Text secondary: `oklch(58% 0.015 248)`
  - Galaxy 1 accent (Data): Scientific blue `oklch(62% 0.20 235)`
  - Galaxy 2 accent (Prompts): Expedition gold `oklch(72% 0.16 72)`
  - Experienced sub-accent: Mint `oklch(72% 0.12 175)`
  - Newcomers sub-accent: Coral `oklch(68% 0.15 20)`
- **Fonts:** Chakra Petch (headings/labels — precise, technical, unmistakably unique) + Jost (body, data labels — geometric, clean, not in monoculture)
- **Motion:** Purposeful — staggered entry on galaxy navigation, smooth panel slide. No bounce. No elastic. Decelerate naturally.

### Design Principles
1. **Credible first, fun second.** Every element should pass the "would this embarrass us in front of Microsoft?" test before the "is this delightful?" test.
2. **Reduce the glow, increase the precision.** Replace neon box-shadow stacks with sharper, more intentional light cues. Less disco, more radar screen.
3. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** The data is interesting — let it breathe. Strong hierarchy in panels (stat → insight → bars → tags) with tight, purposeful spacing.
4. **Two galaxies, clear mission.** Simplify the landing to two large interactive objects. Less choice paralysis, cleaner visual composition.
5. **No banned patterns.** Zero `border-left` accent stripes. Zero gradient text. Zero pure black or pure white.
